Process

From your kitchen to a Nepalese farm

Five steps, one Batch ID, full traceability. Here's exactly what happens to your segregated waste after we collect it.

The journey

The five step path of a batch

Every batch moves through these five steps. Customers receive an SMS update at each stage.

Step 1 · At your business

1. Business segregates waste

Your staff separate organic (food scraps, peelings) from recyclable (plastic, paper, glass, metal) at source. Clean separation at source is what makes everything downstream possible.

Step 2 · Pickup at your door

2. Field worker collects (Batch ID generated)

Our collector arrives on schedule, weighs the segregated waste and generates a unique Batch ID like WM-2026-004283. You receive an SMS with this ID.

Step 3 · Sorting Facility, Itahari

3. Sorting facility segregation

At our Itahari facility, trained sorters separate the waste into clean organic and recyclable streams. Contamination is removed; recyclables are baled by material type.

Step 4 · Compost Plant, Itahari

4. Composting / recycling

Organic waste is loaded into windrows at our Itahari plant for a 14 day composting cycle. Recyclable streams are sent to certified recycling partners in Itahari.

Step 5 · Partner farm

5. Compost delivered to farm

Matured compost is weighed and delivered to a partner farm. You receive a final SMS confirming how much compost was delivered and to which farm.
