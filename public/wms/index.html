How it works

From your kitchen to a farm, in three steps

Segregation at source is the heart of our model. We keep it simple, traceable and rewarding for every business we serve.

01

Collect

Our field workers collect segregated waste from your business on a schedule that suits you. Every pickup generates a Batch ID for full traceability.

02

Segregate

At our Itahari sorting facility, waste is split into organic and recyclable streams, clean separation is what makes real recycling possible.

03

Compost

Organic waste is composted at our Itahari plant and delivered to partner farms. Recyclables go to certified recycling partners.

The journey

A four stage path, fully traceable

Each batch of waste moves through four clearly defined stages. Customers get an SMS update at every step.

1
Collection
2
Sorting
3
Composting
4
Farm Delivery

1 · Collection

Field worker arrives on schedule, weighs and loads segregated waste, opens a Batch ID.

2 · Sorting

At our Itahari facility, organic is separated from recyclable streams for clean downstream processing.

3 · Composting

Organic waste is windrow composted at our Itahari plant over a 14 day cycle.

4 · Farm Delivery

Matured compost is weighed and delivered to a partner farm. Recyclables go to certified partners.
