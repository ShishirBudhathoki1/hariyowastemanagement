About Us

We close the loop on waste in the Itahari

Hariyo Waste is a Nepalese waste management startup that buys segregated waste from businesses, recycles it responsibly, and turns organic waste into compost for local farms.

Our story

Started with one simple observation

Every day, hotels, restaurants and cafés across Itahari throw away hundreds of kilograms of valuable organic and recyclable waste, while local farms pay for chemical fertilisers. The loop is broken.

Hariyo Waste, "hariyo" means "green" in Nepali, was founded in 2025 to close that loop. We work with businesses to segregate at source, collect on schedule, sort at our Itahari facility, and turn organic waste into windrow compost at our Itahari plant. The compost is then delivered to partner farms in Itahari.

Every pickup gets a Batch ID, so our customers can follow their waste from kitchen to compost. No black boxes, just clean, traceable, Nepali grown compost.

Our mission

Make segregation at source the default for every Nepali business, and turn the resulting waste into value for farms, not landfills.

What drives us

Mission, vision & values

Three principles that shape every collection, every batch and every delivery we make.

Mission

To divert as much segregated waste from Itahari landfills as possible, and return it to the soil of Nepalese farms.

Vision

A place where every business segregates, every batch is traceable, and every farm has access to affordable, locally made compost.

Values

Segregation at source. Full transparency. Fair pay for field workers. Respect for the people and the soil of Nepal.
